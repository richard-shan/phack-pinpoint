# Pinpoint responder
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
ios
## Stack
Swift and SwiftUI, native MapKit, WKWebView hosting the existing Matterport Bundle SDK, iOS 17 or newer.
## Users
First responders demonstrating the journey from dispatch to a caller inside a building.
## Product Purpose
Keep the map central while showing the destination, staging entrance, and indoor route.
## Capabilities and Constraints
The user confirmed a self-contained sample incident. The incident and route are simulated; the indoor building is a real Matterport scan. The viewer assets and route are bundled; imagery requires internet. Dollhouse and first-person views share the dispatcher’s red-arrow scene component. No live dispatch or real GPS navigation. Installation on an iPhone requires the user's Apple signing team.
## Brand Commitments
Extend Pinpoint's existing safety-wayfinding identity in app/DESIGN.md using native iOS controls.
